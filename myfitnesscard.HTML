Fitness Routine Cards

In this page it will show a simple weekly fitness routine.

Stretching

In the start of the day do some simple stretches for your arms, legs, back, and neck.

Time: 10 minutes (at least)

Cardio

you should do jumping jacks, jogging, or skipping to improve stamina and energy.

Time: 20 minutes(at least)

Strength

Try practise squats, push-ups, and planks to build body strength and core mucle

Time: 15 minutes (at most)

Yoga

You should try simple yoga poses to improve balance, flexibility, and your focus.

Time: 15 minutes (at most)

Walk in the evening

You should go for a relaxing walk to stay active and refresh your mind after a long day outside.

Time: 25 minutes (at least)

water and rest

Drink enough water to refull your body and take proper rest after your workout.

Time: All day any day

NOTE: A good routine includes exercise, rest, healthy food, and consistency. NOT A ONE TIME THING
